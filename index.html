Album Descrption:

The Weeknd, born Abel Tesfaye, is a Canadian singer, songwriter, and producer. He is known for his atmospheric sound, emotional intensity, and genre-blending style.

Rising from his early 2011 mixtapes House of Balloons, Thursday, and Echoes of Silence, he quickly became one of the most influential artists in modern R&B and pop.

His breakout albums Kiss Land (2013), Beauty Behind the Madness (2015), and Starboy (2016) earned global success.

Since then, he has released acclaimed projects like After Hours (2020) and Dawn FM (2022), each known for cinematic storytelling and chart topping singles.
